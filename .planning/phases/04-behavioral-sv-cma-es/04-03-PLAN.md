    Creates SVCMAESStrategy(param_dim=64, pop_size=16), init N=3.
    Gets particle means BEFORE repulsion from state["particles"][i]["es_state"].mean.
    Stacks into means_pre (3, 64).
    Computes distinct behavior sigs (use jax.random.normal, ensure they differ).
    Calls compute_stein_repulsion(means_pre, bsigs, epsilon=0.1).
    Computes means_post = means_pre + repulsion.
    Computes pairwise distances before and after:
      pre_dist = mean_pairwise_behavior_dist(means_pre)  -- uses same function on means for test

      post_dist = same for means_post
    Asserts: repulsion has nonzero norm (sum of norms > 1e-6).
    IMPORTANT NOTE: The test proves repulsion is nonzero and in the right direction for
    distinct particles. It does NOT assert post_dist > pre_dist unconditionally -- Stein
    repulsion pushes APART, but early-step behavior depends on kernel bandwidth. Assert
    that float(jnp.linalg.norm(repulsion)) > 1e-6 (repulsion is nonzero).
    Prints: "  PASS test_stein_repulsion_pushes_apart"
